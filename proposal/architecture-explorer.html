Team fit

A small, growing team · 1 AI engineer plus developers & UX/UI designers — exact headcount not fixed here, and it doesn't need to be. Onboarding is a guided self-service flow, so none of the three paths assumes a set team size: more people just means more replicas and, eventually, moving up a path (Lean → Self-Managed → Managed), not a redesign.
